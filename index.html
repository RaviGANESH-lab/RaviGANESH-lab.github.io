About

Dr. Ravi GANESH is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at IIT Ropar, where he has led the ECO Geotechnics Lab since 2023.

The ECO Geotechnics Lab's focus lies at the intersection of computational geomechanics, energy geotechnics, and offshore foundation engineering, with an emphasis on developing advanced analytical and numerical methods for geotechnical engineering.

The lab is dedicated to advancing the science and engineering of geotechnical systems through theoretical developments, computational modelling, and numerical methods. Our research addresses challenging problems in offshore anchoring systems, foundation engineering, earth pressures, slope stability, and geotechnical infrastructure under static, cyclic, and seismic loading. We develop innovative methodologies based on plasticity theory, finite element limit analysis, the slip line method, and other advanced computational techniques to deliver reliable, efficient, and practical solutions for modern geotechnical engineering.

Research Focus
Foundations & Bearing Capacity
Bearing Capacity & Foundation Stability
Shallow and deep foundations under eccentric, oblique, and uplift loading; bearing capacity on slopes and layered strata; foundation stability under combined vertical, horizontal and uplift forces.
Slopes & Retaining Structures
Slope Stability & Retaining Wall Stability
Static and seismic slope stability, active/passive earth pressure on retaining walls, and earth pressure on buried and trench structures under heterogeneous backfill conditions.
Unsaturated & Seepage
Unsaturated Soils & Seepage
Earth pressure and slope response in unsaturated backfills, seepage and pore-pressure analysis, and the influence of groundwater fluctuation on foundation and anchor capacity.
Offshore & Energy Geotechnics
Offshore Anchors & Foundation Systems
Plate anchors, suction caissons and mooring foundations for offshore energy infrastructure; capacity under combined VHM loading, cyclic effects, and seismic forces.
Constitutive Behaviour
Soil Anisotropy, Nonlinearity & Non-homogeneity
Strength and stiffness models for anisotropic, non-homogeneous and nonlinear (power-law) soils, and their influence on classical bearing capacity, anchor and slope stability solutions.
Computational Geomechanics
Finite Element Limit Analysis
Upper- and lower-bound FELA, stress characteristics, and kinematic mechanisms — advanced numerical tools for bearing capacity, earth pressure and slope stability problems.
